## Research implementations

- **[VEGA-3D](https://github.com/H-EmbodVis/VEGA-3D)** — Implicit 3D priors from video generation models for spatial understanding and embodied tasks. [Project page](https://h-embodvis.github.io/VEGA-3D/).
- **[TurboVLA](https://github.com/H-EmbodVis/TurboVLA)** — A compact vision-language-action model for efficient robotic manipulation. [Project page](https://h-embodvis.github.io/TurboVLA/).
- **[GaugeAlign](https://github.com/HyperbolicCurve/GaugeAlign-AICity2026)** — The winning solution for AI City Challenge 2026, Track 1. Inference-time coordinate canonicalization for multi-camera 3D perception.
